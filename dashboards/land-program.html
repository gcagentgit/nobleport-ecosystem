Thesis

Why land

Finite by definition

No new land is created. Parcels at the urban edge of a growing region change use over decades, and that change is driven by zoning, infrastructure and demand, not by a building cycle NoblePort controls.

Operator knowledge

NoblePort Construction LLC has worked Essex County sites for 35+ years. Permit history, soil, wetlands, access and utility realities are things the operating company already knows, which is the diligence that matters for raw land.

Transparent holding

An SPV per parcel, a registry contract that records deed hash, attestation and issuance, and public documents anchored to IPFS/Arweave. Holders can verify what is held, by whom, and under what restrictions.

Land also carries real risks: illiquidity, carrying costs and taxes, zoning that never changes, environmental findings, and the possibility of total loss. None of that is reduced by tokenization. A token changes how an interest is recorded and transferred, not what the land is worth.

Target framework

Allocation categories

Target mix for a program that does not yet hold any parcel. These are planning categories, not holdings, and they will change with counsel and market conditions.

Transitional land target 40%

Parcels at the urban-rural boundary where zoning change or infrastructure extension is plausible on a multi-year horizon.

Agricultural land target 25%

Working farmland with water rights, leased to operators. Lease income, if any, would be disclosed in offering documents, not promised here.

Timberland target 20%

Managed forest with selective harvest cycles; carbon programs evaluated case by case.

Entitled development parcels target 15%

Entitled but unbuilt lots with infrastructure access, which the construction company can take vertical under written scope.

Mechanics

How it would work

1

Acquisition and diligence

Title search, survey, environmental assessment, wetlands and zoning review. Evidence hashes are what later go on-chain; documents stay off-chain.

2

SPV formation

Each parcel is deeded to its own bankruptcy-remote SPV (a separate issuer entity, to be formed with counsel). Independent custody and audit controls apply. The construction company is a contractor to the SPV, not its owner.

3

Register, attest, issue

An agent records the deed hash and fraction count in PropertyRegistry.sol. A second, distinct verifier attests title and appraisal evidence. Only then are fractions issued, to a verified custody wallet.

4

Identity-gated transfers

Fractions move only between wallets verified in the ERC-3643 Identity Registry, subject to the compliance module (jurisdiction blocks, holder caps, holding periods). Unverified recipients are rejected on-chain.

5

Reporting and distributions

Appraisal updates and milestone anchors are on-chain events. Any distribution of lease or sale proceeds would follow the SPV's operating agreement and be settled in USDC on the payment rail or in fiat; terms belong in counsel-reviewed documents.
